What this is

A research prototype for criterion-level clinical trial eligibility screening. A Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct model fine-tuned with LoRA on the TrialGPT criterion annotations (37 training patients) predicts, for each criterion, a label (included / not included / excluded / not excluded / not enough information / not applicable) and the supporting sentence IDs. Explanations are built from the cited sentences, never invented.

Overall outcome

Decoding paths

Uncached greedy reference, KV-cached greedy decoder (explicit cache, position ids and mask) and greedy speculative decoding with a fine-tuned 0.5B draft. All three are implemented in this repository and do not call generate().

Known limitations

  • Rare labels (not included, excluded) are rarely predicted correctly.
  • Missing information is often mistaken for "not excluded"; decisions without cited evidence are flagged.
  • Trained on short notes (max 562 tokens). Plain-text notes are split with a simple rule that can mis-split abbreviations.
  • CPU inference is slow: roughly 10-60 s per criterion depending on the machine.
